77U standard cover model — Real-ESRGAN general x4v3

The normal-quality Core ML model is a blend of two official checkpoints,
25% general and 75% weak-denoise. 77U uses a compiled Core ML conversion
with FP16 and 8-bit weight palettization. The original project license is
reproduced in RealESRGAN-LICENSE.txt.
